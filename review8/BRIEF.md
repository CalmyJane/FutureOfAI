## What to check, per chapter
1. **Read it as a reader would.** Note where it is unclear, repetitive or padded, where an argument does not follow, and where tone is unfair to a named person or company.
2. **Check the facts against sources.** Use web search and fetch to check numbers, dates, quotations (word for word), attributions and legal statements. Concentrate on:
   - 2025–2026 material and anything about named companies or people;
   - quotations;
   - anything that could be defamatory or legally sensitive.

   The book says it is current to late November 2026. Flag anything that was already out of date then, or that changed after that point and up to today.
3. **Recompute worked examples and arithmetic.**
4. **Check sources.** Spot-check that cited bib entries exist and support the sentence. Look for invented or mismatched sources.
5. **Check internal consistency** with other chapters: the same fact given differently, and cross-references that point to the wrong section.

## Whole-book checks
- **The AI-authorship disclosure.** Look at the preface ("How the book was made"), the copyright page and the back cover. Is it clear, honest and sufficient? Does any statement overclaim, for example about checking or human review?
- **Front matter and production.** Check the title page, the copyright page and the imprint. In the PDF, look for:
  - text running off the page;
  - large blank gaps;
  - broken figures;
  - index entries pointing to wrong pages (sample 30).

  On the cover, check that the spine width matches the page count: BoD white paper is 0.12 mm per leaf, plus 0.6 mm.

## Output
Write your reports as files, chapter by chapter as you finish each, so that nothing is lost if you are interrupted:
- `review8/r-chNN.md` for each chapter, and `review8/r-production.md` for the whole-book checks.
- Use this structure: `## Verdict`, `## MUST change before printing`, `## SHOULD change`, `## Could not verify`, `## Checked and confirmed` (a brief list), and a tally (claims checked / confirmed / wrong / outdated).
- Write each item as:

  ```
  ### M1. <title>
  - Where: textbook/chapters/chNN.md:<line>
  - Text now: "<exact text>"

  - Evidence: <URL + exact quoted words from the source, with date>
  - Change to: "<exact replacement text>" (plus any new bib entry in the style of the existing ones)
  ```

- **MUST** means wrong, misquoted, legally risky, or misleading. **SHOULD** means real but not blocking. Do not pad the report with taste-only items.
