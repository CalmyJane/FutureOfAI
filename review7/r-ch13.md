## Verdict
Ready to print. The chapter is accurate and carefully hedged. I checked about 70 claims against primary or reliable secondary sources. ## SHOULD change

- Where: textbook/chapters/ch13.md:290
- Text now: "In October at least eight more states sued OpenAI ... Texas followed on 23 October and Oregon on 17 November, bringing the number of states suing OpenAI over harms to children to eleven by mid-November 2026"
- Problem: INCONSISTENT. Florida plus "at least eight" plus Texas and Oregon makes at least eleven, not exactly eleven. OPB's 17 November report gives a firm "eight", so the hedge is the only problem.
- Evidence: https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/17/oregon-ag-sues-openai-claims-company-knowingly-endangers-teens/ (17 Nov 2026): "Eight U.S. states sued OpenAI in October, each claiming the technology is dangerously influential on teens."
- Change to: "In October eight more states sued OpenAI" (keep "eleven"), or keep "at least eight" and write "to at least eleven".
