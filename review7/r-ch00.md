# Review 7: Preface (textbook/chapters/ch00-preface.md)

## Verdict
Ready to print. The preface is accurate about the book's structure. Its AI-authorship disclosure is clear and does not overclaim: it says most of the text was generated by AI, that the checking was also done by AI, and that no independent human expert has reviewed the book. Two small wording points are listed below. Neither blocks printing.

## MUST change before printing
None.

## SHOULD change

### S1. "Were corrected before printing" depends on this final round being applied
- Where: textbook/chapters/ch00-preface.md:52
- Text now: "The errors and out-of-date statements these rounds found were corrected before printing, except where a finding was judged on re-checking to be mistaken."
- Problem: UNCLEAR. The sentence is true only if the findings of this seventh round are also applied, or each one is explicitly rejected, before the files go to BoD. If any finding is left open on purpose (for example a deferred SHOULD item), the sentence overclaims slightly.
- Evidence: internal. The sentence describes the process, and this round is part of it.
- Change to: no change, provided every finding from this round is either applied or rejected. If some are deliberately left open, use: "Most of the errors and out-of-date statements these rounds found were corrected before printing; findings judged on re-checking to be mistaken, and a few minor points of wording, were left as they were."

### S2. "Every paragraph that states a fact cites its sources" is slightly too absolute
- Where: textbook/chapters/ch00-preface.md:5
- Text now: "Every paragraph that states a fact cites its sources."
- Problem: UNCLEAR, a mild overclaim. The preface itself states facts without citations, for example "In AI and in economics, many important results first appear this way" (line 34). Bridging and summary paragraphs in the chapters also state facts without citations, such as the closing paragraph of Section 1.8, which relies on citations given earlier in the chapter. A reader who checks will find exceptions.
- Evidence: ch00-preface.md:34; ch01.md:268 is an opinion labelled as the author's, so it is consistent with the rules.
- Change to: "Every paragraph in the chapters that reports a finding cites its sources."
