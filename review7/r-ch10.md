## Checked and confirmed
- HEPI Student Generative AI Survey 2026 (PDF): 1,054 full-time undergraduates, polled by Savanta in December 2025; 94% use AI for assessed work; AI text included directly in assessed work went from 3% (2024) to 8% (2025) to 12% (2026); 65% say assessment has changed significantly.
- State suits against OpenAI:
  - Florida, 1 June 2026;
  - eight states, 14 Oct 2026;
  - Texas;
  - Oregon, 17 Nov 2026, filed in Multnomah County under the Unlawful Trade Practices Act.
  That makes eleven by mid-November, matching the text.
- New York companion law: signed Wednesday 28 Oct 2026 (AP); defines "unsafe AI companion features"; Attorney General enforcement; effective 1 January. Article 47 has been in effect since 5 Nov 2025.
- NYC Public Schools, 2 Sept 2026: one-year moratorium on student-facing generative AI from 2-K to grade 8, companion chatbots barred in all grades, and pilots allowed in high school. The book's "pre-school" fairly renders "2-K".
- Garante, 9 July 2026: €158,000 fine on Character Technologies for its privacy notice and age verification. It ordered age checks that work and minors' profiles set to private by default.
- Tribunale di Roma no. 4153/2026: the €15m ChatGPT fine was annulled on competence (the one-stop shop after OpenAI Ireland was recognised in February 2024), without ruling on the merits. This is consistent with Section 13.6.
- MASAI final results (Lund University): 82 versus 93 interval cancers, 12% fewer, and higher sensitivity without more false positives. This is consistent with 1.55 versus 1.76 per 1,000 and a ratio of 0.88.
- Penda Health trial (*Nature Medicine*, 2026): 16 clinics, 9,691 patients, April–July 2025; no reduction in 14-day treatment failure; "any benefit … probably modest".
- Character.AI and Google settlement (CNN, Jan 2026): five suits.
- Turkle, *Artificial Intimacy: Who We Become When We Talk to Machines* (Little, Brown, 29 Sept 2026): the book exists, and its argument is as described.
