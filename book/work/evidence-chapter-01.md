- **Reg. 11 "600 s"**: correct as the default; the regulation also requires capability up to 1800 s. Chapter mentions both.
- **Total GW lost in Iberia (25.6 GW Spain / 5.9 GW Portugal)**: secondary source only; not used.

- **Final adopted LFDD numbers** in Regulation 2017/2196 not re-read; the chapter uses the 49.0 Hz start and roughly 45% by 48.0 Hz, which is consistent with national implementations (Elia: 45%).
