## Searched for and not found / not used
- **How often governments or campaigns consult simulated citizens versus real ones** (the "consulted more often" claim): no measurement found anywhere. The chapter states this plainly and proposes "consultation share" as a measurable quantity.
- **Any jurisdiction requiring disclosure of synthetic-respondent use in public decisions or campaigns:** none found. EU 2024/900 covers ads, not message rehearsal.
- **Use of synthetic electorates in Swiss campaigns:** nothing found; the Geneva vignette is fiction.
- **Geneva 2025 turnout and postal-vote share:** not found in search results; not used. (A June 2025 Grand Conseil motion, M 3136, notes falling participation and that postal voting is fixed by federal law; not used.)
