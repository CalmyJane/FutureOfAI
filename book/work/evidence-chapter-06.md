## Searched for and not found / not used
- **"100× the volume" of approvals (outline):** no measurement of how many approvals a person makes per day, before or after agents. Stated in the text as unmeasured; the 100× is not used as a fact.
- **A measured "signature capacity"** (how many items a professional can genuinely verify per hour) for discharge summaries or similar documents: not found. Proposed as a measurement (seeded-error detection).
- **Evaluation results of the Chelsea and Westminster discharge pilot:** none found as of search date.
- **A 49% "lower bound" from a newer source:** the 49–96% range is van der Sijs 2006 specifically; the 2020 review gives 46.2–96.2%. Both cited with their own numbers.
- A preprint meta-analysis giving a pooled DDI override rate of 79.8% (44 studies) was found but not used because it is a preprint.
