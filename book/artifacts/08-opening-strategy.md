# Opening strategy

**Strategy:** open in 2036, in a kitchen, with a small domestic request. The reader should feel the comfort of the decade before the catastrophe. Then switch to the author's voice in 2026 with the claim: "No agent malfunctioned."

**Candidate first lines:**
1. "At 19:41 on the fourteenth of February, Hanne Albrecht asked her house to make the music room warm."
2. "The cheapest minute of the evening was going to be 19:45, and every house in Europe knew it."
3. "Nobody in Altwerder remembers the blackout starting; they remember the violin stopping."
4. "Ottilie had been told, very clearly, that the grid would randomise her."

**Chosen:** 1, with line 2 as the closing beat of the first section.

**What the first page promises:** a lived-in 2036 and a puzzle with a mechanism, and an author who explains instead of preaching.

**How the prologue ends:** "That button is where this book begins." The override switch, as a concrete object.
