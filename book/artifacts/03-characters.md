# Recurring figures (2036 vignettes)

Nonfiction adaptation: the book has no plot characters. Five recurring people carry the 2036 vignettes, so readers live in the decade. Each vignette is 400 to 700 words, in the present tense, and clearly marked as a scenario. One-off figures are allowed in other chapters. Every figure has a contradiction, because a scenario populated by saints reads as advertising.

## Hanne Albrecht (74), Altwerder, a village east of Leipzig (fictional)
- Retired teacher of German and music. Widowed in 2033; her husband Klaus died of ALS.
- Her agent: **Ottilie**, a fiduciary household agent, model line "Linde-7" (fictional).
- Klaus's agent, **Bruno**, still runs, managing the small trust Klaus left for their grandchildren. This drives chapter 5 (custody) and chapter 14 (the dead's share).
- Wound: she watched Klaus lose his speech. She fears being unable to say no.
- Desire: to keep her independence. Need: to admit she relies on Ottilie.
- Contradiction: she lectures her grandchildren about "thinking for yourself" and approves 140 agent requests a week without reading them (chapter 6).
- Voice card: dry, Saxon understatement, music metaphors, short sentences when annoyed. She calls Ottilie "Fräulein".
- Appears in: the prologue (her heat pump at 19:42), chapters 5, 6, 13 and 16, and the epilogue.

## Teo Brandão (24 in 2036), Porto
- Part of the class of 2033. He graduated in data journalism into the missing entry rung, and no newsroom was hiring juniors.
- Since 2034 he has served in the Portuguese grid **Competence Reserve** (Reserva de Competências): paid, drilled, and qualified to operate substations by hand.
- Wound: his father, a lineman, was told his skills were obsolete in 2027.
- Contradiction: he joined for the stipend and is half ashamed of it. On the night it matters, he is the only one in his shift who remembers the paper procedure.
- Voice card: quick, ironic, football analogies. He calls the agents "os simpáticos" (the nice ones).
- Appears in: the prologue (aftermath), chapters 1 and 4, and the epilogue.

## Ravi Menon (38), Bengaluru
- A top-decile professional rater ("evaluation specialist") since 2027, who has worked for four vendors serving five labs.
- He discovers his own phrasings and preferences echoed across models he never rated. This drives chapter 2 (the Funnel).
- Wound: a career built on judgement he was never credited for.
- Contradiction: he is proud of his taste and frightened by its reach.
- Voice card: precise, a little formal, cricket and Carnatic music references.
- Appears in: chapters 2, 7 and 17.

## Wanjiru Kamau (41), Nairobi
- A school administrator and mother of two. A ward-level citizen auditor via her agent, which reads the county budget line by line (chapter 8).
- Kenya in 2036 runs geothermal-powered inference parks at Olkaria and is a net exporter of "thought" (chapter 12).
- Wound: she grew up watching budgets disappear.
- Contradiction: she uses her agent to expose the county, and resents the county using its agents to model her.
- Voice card: brisk, funny, Sheng words in dialogue, impatient with jargon.
- Appears in: chapters 8, 9, 10, 12 and the epilogue.

## Mara Oyelaran (49), Geneva
- A Nigerian-Swiss statistician, formerly a bank risk modeller (she started in 2008). In 2036 she heads the Concordance desk at a fictional international body, the **Bureau for Correlated Risk (BCR)**, set up in 2034.
- She publishes the quarterly Concordance Index (chapter 3) and testifies after the Long Minute.
- Wound: she saw 2008 coming in a spreadsheet and said nothing loudly enough.
- Contradiction: she preaches diversity of models and uses one model for everything at home.
- Voice card: dry, exact, finance metaphors, low tolerance for "unprecedented".
- Appears in: chapters 1, 3, 7 and 17.

## The narrator ("I")
The author: a curious, sceptical writer in 2026 who has read the research and doesn't oversell it. They are present as a guide, not as a character with a plot. They admit uncertainty, and they say "I think" when it is opinion and "the study found" when it is fact.
