## Comps (from recall; provisional hypotheses unless a source is given)
1. *Nexus*, Yuval Noah Harari, 2024: big-history framing of information networks and AI. We share the conceptual ambition; we differ in horizon (ten years, not millennia) and in testable concepts.
2. *Co-Intelligence*, Ethan Mollick, 2024: accessible, evidence-led. Among the most-shelved AI books on Goodreads (goodreads.com/shelf/show/ai, checked 2026-10-20). We share the readability.
3. *The Coming Wave*, Mustafa Suleyman, 2023: the policy-oriented reader. Also among the most-shelved on Goodreads (same source).
