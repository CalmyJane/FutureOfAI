## Long Minute: fact base
- German 50.2 Hz problem: a fixed disconnect threshold meant up to about 14 GW of PV could trip together. At least 315,000 systems were retrofitted for €65–170M (DNV; IEEE Spectrum).
- UK Smart Charge Points Regulations 2021, reg. 11: chargers must apply a random delay (up to 1800 s; 600 s default per guidance), and **the owner can override it**.
- Iberian blackout, 28 April 2025: the ENTSO-E final report (March 2026) found multi-factor voltage-control failures and a ~90-second cascade. It was not agent-caused.
