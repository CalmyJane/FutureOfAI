# Review: Chapter 6, AI for Science, Mathematics and Engineering

## Verdict
Ready after one fix. The chapter is careful and well sourced. It says plainly that its showcase results come mostly from Google DeepMind, and that is fair. The long Navier–Stokes case study treats OpenAI, Buckmaster and Alpöge even-handedly: OpenAI's denials are quoted in full, and Buckmaster's sharper allegations in his statement are left out rather than amplified. Anthropic's own claims (the zeta zeros result, and the Riemann hypothesis claim by Alpöge, an Anthropic researcher) are labelled as claims and get critical quotations. There is one MUST, and it is in Anthropic-related material: the account of Alpöge's Riemann hypothesis claim on 20 November 2026 was overtaken within a day. The statement check the book calls "pending" passed, the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative certified both the statement and the proof on 21 November, and the claim also covers GRH for Dirichlet L-functions. For a book current to late November 2026, this needs updating.
