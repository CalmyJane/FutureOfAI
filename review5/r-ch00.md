# Review 5: Preface (textbook/chapters/ch00-preface.md)

## Verdict
Clear, well organised, and honest about how far it can vouch for the text. The AI-authorship disclosure (lines 50–56) is prominent ("Most of this book was generated by AI"). It says plainly that no independent human expert has reviewed the whole book, and it puts responsibility on the author. One statement about the review process will be out of date once this fifth round is complete. Ready after one MUST fix.

## MUST change before printing

### M1. Number of review rounds is now wrong
- Where: textbook/chapters/ch00-preface.md:52
- Text now: "Four such review rounds were run. In the third, about 2,600 statements were checked against their sources; the fourth, a final check before printing, re-examined the facts in every chapter."
- Problem: OUTDATED. A fifth review round (this one, brief dated 26 November 2026) is being run before printing. "Four" is therefore wrong, and calling the fourth round "a final check before printing" is no longer accurate. This is a statement about how the book was checked, so it should be exact.
- Evidence: review5/BRIEF.md, "Fifth review: brief (26 November 2026) ... Your review is the last check before the author prints it."
- Change to: "Five such review rounds were run. In the third, about 2,600 statements were checked against their sources; the fourth and fifth, final checks before printing, re-examined the facts in every chapter."

## SHOULD change

### S1. Readers are pointed to logs they cannot see
- Where: textbook/chapters/ch00-preface.md:52
- Text now: "claims that rest on them are listed as unverified in the project's working logs."
- Problem: UNCLEAR. The working logs are not published, so a reader cannot use this pointer. As written, it can read as an assurance the reader has no means of checking.
- Evidence: Nothing in the preface, the front matter or the reference list says where the logs can be found.
- Change to: "claims that rest on them are listed as unverified in the project's working logs, which are not published with the book but are available from the author on request." (Or, if they will not be available: "...which are not published.")

## Could not verify
- Line 52: "chiefly Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.2". Both models exist. Opus 5.5 was announced on 22 September 2026, and press reports (for example 9to5Mac, 9 November 2026, https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-fable-5-2-model-details-here/) date Fable 5.2's release to 9 November 2026. Fable 5.2 was therefore available only for the last few weeks of the work. If earlier drafting or checking used earlier models (for example Fable 5.1), "chiefly ... Fable 5.2" may overstate. The author should confirm this, or add "and earlier versions of these models".
- Line 52: "about 2,600 statements were checked" in the third round. The reviewer was instructed not to read the earlier review files.

## Checked and confirmed
- "more than 1,200 references" (line 5): the built PDF's reference list ends at entry 1,306.
- The descriptions of the five parts and fourteen chapters match the chapter contents and titles. Chapter 2 contains the worked backpropagation and convolution examples, and Chapter 14 contains the ten proposals.
- The Chapter 3 tokenisation and probability tables are cited as an example of author-computed numbers. They are consistent with the Chapter 3 section list.
- The disclosure states that the author did not write most of the sentences and that no independent human expert has reviewed the whole book. It also states the conflict of interest (the models' maker is a subject of the book) and that errors remain. None of it overclaims, apart from M1.
