# Alternative titles (searched 26 November 2026)

The current title, *Understanding AI*, is generic and is also the name of Timothy B. Lee's newsletter. These candidates turned up no existing book, newsletter or podcast with the same title in web searches. Search cannot prove a title is free: self-published books are often not indexed. Before deciding, search the exact title on Amazon.com, Amazon.de and the Deutsche Nationalbibliothek catalogue (portal.dnb.de), and run a trademark search (EUIPO, DPMA, USPTO).

| # | Title and subtitle | Why it fits | What the search found |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Machine Explains Itself:** How AI Works, Where It Stands, and the Decade Ahead | Works two ways: an explainer, and a book written by AI | No book. There is a Substack post "The Machine That Explains Itself", and similar phrases occur in explainable-AI papers. Low risk. |
| 2 | **By Its Own Account:** Artificial Intelligence — How It Works, What It Changes, What Comes Next | A quiet nod to the AI authorship; reads as evidence and testimony | Appears unused: no book, newsletter or podcast. |
| 3 | **Self-Portrait of a Machine:** AI's Technology, Frontier, Risks and the Decade Ahead | The strongest nod to the AI authorship, and literary | No book. A 2025 video artwork, "Self-Portrait" by Ben Cullen Williams, has Gemini describing itself. Thematically close: a mild flag. |
| 4 | **Next Token, Next Decade:** How AI Models Work and Where They Are Taking Us | Memorable and technically literate | No match for the full phrase. Several podcasts and a newsletter are called "The Next Token", so only the full phrase is distinctive. |
| 5 | **What the Models Know:** An Evidence-Based Guide to AI and the Decade Ahead | Sober and evidence-first | Appears unused. |

Dropped: *Machine Testimony* (Yale Law Journal article, 2017), *Written by the Machine* (crowded "AI wrote this" niche), *Weights and Measures* (Joseph Roth novel), *The Decade of the Machine* (generic), *The Sober Machine*.

To change the title, edit `TITLE`/`SUBTITLE` in `textbook/print/build.py` and the title text in `textbook/print/cover.py`. Then rebuild both.
