## Fact-check (2026-10-22)

Independent check of about 120 numbers, dates, quotations and attributions against primary sources (Crossref/PubMed abstracts, arXiv API, OUP full text, NBER/BFI/Census PDFs, METR, PwC, ILO, Goldman Sachs, Yale Budget Lab, Minneapolis Fed and Anthropic pages).

**Writer's flags**
1. Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond (QJE 2025): the text says "quasi-experimental", "staggered" roll-out and difference-in-differences, which is correct. The published figures are 15% on average and 36% for the lowest skill quintile, from the OUP full text: "lowest skill quintile ... increase of 0.5 in RPH, or 36%". The 14%/34% figures belong to the 2023 NBER version and are not used. Also confirmed: Fortune 500, small and medium-sized US businesses, 89% of agents outside the US, GPT-3, fall 2020–winter 2021, the 2-month vs 6-month tenure comparison, and the four alternative estimators. No change.
2. Dell'Acqua et al., Org. Sci. 37(2):403–423 (March 2026): the Crossref abstract gives 758, three arms, 18 tasks, 12.2%, 25.1%, "significantly improved quality" and "19% less likely to produce correct solutions", all as in the text. No change.
3. Humlum & Vestergaard: the BFI WP 2025-56 PDF (dated 9 May 2025) gives "precise zeros", rules out effects >1% and reports 2.8% time savings, so the May 2025 version and the 1% figure are correctly attached. NBER w33777 (revised March 2026, "Still Waters, Rapid Currents") gives "ruling out effects larger than 2% two years after the launch of ChatGPT", correctly attached in both places. Bib year for humlum2025still changed 2025 → 2026 so the in-text label matches the March 2026 revision the text describes, and the NBER URL was added.
4. Cui et al. CI: 26.08 ± 1.96 × 10.3 = 5.89 to 46.27, which rounds to +5.9 to +46.3. Correct, and "approx." is kept. The abstract gives SE "10.3%".
5.
