### Writer's flags
1. **53% vs 28.3%**: Confirmed in AI Index 2026, ch. 4 (PDF pp. 198–199): 53% within three years comes from the genaiadoptiontracker.com / Harvard Project on Workforce series (Bick et al.; Real-Time Population Survey, US adults 18–64). 28.3% (US ranked 24th of top 30, second half 2025) comes from Microsoft AI Economy Institute telemetry, the same section that reports the correlation with GDP per capita. Text rewritten to name both measures, give the 28.3% figure, and explain why they differ.
2. **900M weekly users**: TechCrunch (Aisha Malik, 27 Feb 2026) reports that OpenAI announced it in a blog post accompanying a $110bn funding round (openai.com/index/scaling-ai-for-everyone/). openai.com returned 403, and web.archive.org content is blocked by egress policy; the Wayback availability API shows captures exist (TechCrunch 2026-10-13; OpenAI post 2026-10-15) but they could not be read. The text now labels it as a company figure reported by the press and not independently verified.
3. **o1 timing**: The system card (arXiv 2412.16720v1) is dated 5 Dec 2024 and says o1 was "previously OpenAI o1-preview"; red-teaming ran "through mid-September 2024". Wayback availability API: openai.com/o1/ captured 2024-09-13 00:07 UTC, and openai.com/index/learning-to-reason-with-llms/ captured 2024-09-13, which fits an announcement on 12 Sept 2024 (US time). The chapter gives no month, and "2024" is correct. The spec now notes Sept 2024 (preview) / Dec 2024 (system card).
4. **AI-winter bands**: Mitchell 2021 says the first winter followed the 1973 reports (after the late-1960s/early-1970s spring) and that "the late 1980's marked the beginning of a new AI winter", about five years after 1984. The Wikipedia summary (citing Russell & Norvig 2003; Howe 1994) gives "approximately 1974–1980 and 1987–2000" and notes that sources differ. The first band, 1974–80, is kept. The second band changed from 1988–1993 to 1987–1993, with a faded/open right edge because accounts disagree about the end date. The caption keeps "approximate".
5. **Lighthill**: The report is dated "July 1972" (chilton-computing p001), and the overview page says it "was published early in 1973". Both quotes were verified verbatim. No change.
6. **mitchell2021why**: The full text exists only on arXiv (v2, 28 Apr 2021). GECCO 2021 (doi 10.1145/3449639.3465421) has only a one-page keynote abstract (p. 3). The entry stays an arXiv preprint, and the text now labels it as such at first substantive use. All Mitchell quotes were verified against the arXiv PDF.
7. **EU and OECD wording**: Art. 3(1) is verbatim correct. The Art. 3(63) quote had silently dropped "an AI model, including where such an AI model is", which made self-supervised training look like a definitional requirement. Corrected. The Recital 12 quote "symbolic representations" was corrected to the OJ wording "symbolic representation of the task to be solved". The other Recital 12 quotes are correct. The OECD definition matches the memorandum verbatim (p. 4; repeated p. 6). The 2019 text, the 8 Nov 2023 Council adoption and March 2024 publication (Crossref issued 2024-03-05) were confirmed.
