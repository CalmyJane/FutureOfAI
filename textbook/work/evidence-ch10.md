Independent check against primary sources (Europe PMC/PubMed abstracts, PMC full texts, Crossref, arXiv API, FDA HTML table, garanteprivacy.it, statute texts). About 140 claims checked (numbers, dates, attributions, quotations, bibliographic fields).

### Writer's flags resolved
1. **gommers2026interval (MASAI final)**: Lancet abstract (Europe PMC, PMID 41620232) confirms 105,934 randomised; interval cancer 1.55 vs 1.76 per 1,000; non-inferior proportion ratio 0.88 (0.65–1.18); sensitivity 80.5% vs 73.8% (p=0.031); specificity 98.5% both. Counts 82 vs 93 confirmed in Lund University release. "Not inferior" wording correct. Bib (vol 407, issue 10527, pp 505–514, 12 authors) confirmed. No change.
2. **heinz2025randomized**: abstract (Semantic Scholar copy of NEJM AI abstract) states "waitlist control (WLC; N=104) ... received no app access during the study period but gained access after its conclusion (8 weeks)"; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06013137 control arm "no intervention". Waiting-list description correct. No change.
3. **desimone2025chalkboards**: WPS 11125 PDF read: 0.31 SD total, 0.23 SD English (0.238), ~US$48 per pupil (marginal $9), attendance 72%, 657/671 assigned, 422/337 analysed, 1.5–2 years of schooling, June–July 2024, nine schools, Copilot/GPT-4, pairs, 90-min, max two per week, lottery among volunteers. All confirmed. Paper is the December 2025 updated version (note added to bib). Added authors' caveat that the girls result may reflect one girls-only school.
4. **fda2026artificial**: re-parsed the live FDA HTML table (content current as of 09/04/2026): 1,614 rows; 1995–29 June 2026; per year 2015–2025 = 6, 18, 27, 65, 80, 114, 129, 162, 226, 235, 335 (2026: 181); Radiology 1,230 (76.2%), Cardiovascular 154, Neurology 73. Softened "the list contains almost no tools based on generative AI" → "the list does not yet show which devices, if any, use generative AI" (the list does not tag them, so "almost none" is not verifiable).
5. **garante2024chatgpt**: garanteprivacy.it docweb 10085432 (Italian and English versions) confirms €15m fine, decision No. 755 of 2 Nov 2024, and the note: "Decision No. 755 ... temporarily removed ... following judgment No. 4153/2026 of the Court of Rome. The judgment, published on 18 March 2026, upheld the appeal lodged against the Authority's decision."
