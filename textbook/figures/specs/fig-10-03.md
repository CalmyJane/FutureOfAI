# Figure 10.3 spec

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show that "medical AI" is many different tools entering at different stages of care, each requiring a different kind of evidence.
- **caption:** Figure 10.3: Where AI sits in the clinical workflow. The patient journey runs from intake and history-taking through investigation, diagnosis and treatment decision to documentation and follow-up. Examples of AI tools are shown at each stage, together with the kind of evidence that would show they help. The diagram illustrates that "medical AI" is many different tools, each needing its own evaluation. Diagram by the author.

## content
Layout: three horizontal rows, read left → right.

**Row 1 (main process, boxes joined by right-pointing arrows):**
1. "Intake and history"
2. "Tests and imaging"
3. "Diagnosis"
4. "Treatment decision"
5. "Documentation"
6. "Follow-up"

**Row 2 (below each box, a lighter box labelled "AI tool", connected by a short dashed vertical line):**

4. under "Treatment decision": "Risk alerts"

**Row 3 (below each AI tool, small italic text labelled "Evidence needed"):**
1. "Conversation safety study"
