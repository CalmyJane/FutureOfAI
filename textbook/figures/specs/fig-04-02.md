# fig-04-02

- **type:** scatter (time on x, log-scale y), with labelled points
- **purpose:** Show that the training compute of notable AI systems has grown by more than twenty orders of magnitude since 1957, with much faster growth since the 2010s.
- **caption:** Figure 4.2: Estimated training compute of selected notable AI systems, 1957–2026, on a logarithmic scale. Each gridline is a tenfold increase. Values are Epoch AI estimates; those marked "likely" are expected to be accurate within a factor of 10, and the others within a factor of 3. Source: @epochai2026data.
- **content:**
  - x-axis: "Publication date" (linear, years 1955–2026; ticks every 10 years).
  - y-axis: "Training compute (FLOP, log scale)", from 10^5 to 10^27; gridline at every power of ten, labels at every second or third power (10^6, 10^9, 10^12, 10^15, 10^18, 10^21, 10^24, 10^27).
  - Data points (all from Epoch AI "Data on AI Models", Notable AI Models CSV downloaded 2026-10-22, column "Training compute (FLOP)"; source key epochai2026data for every point). Plot exactly these values:
