# Figure 10.2 spec

- **type:** bar chart
- **purpose:** Show the rapid growth of US FDA authorisations of AI-enabled medical devices, so students see the scale of regulatory activity (and, via the caption, that authorisation is not proof of patient benefit).
- **caption:** Figure 10.2: Number of AI-enabled medical devices authorised by the US FDA each year, 2015–2025, by date of final decision. Authorisations rose more than fiftyfold over the decade, and about three-quarters of all listed devices are in radiology. Nearly all were cleared through the 510(k) pathway, which requires equivalence to an existing device. Counts reflect the FDA list as updated in September 2026; the FDA adds devices whose summaries are published late, so recent years may be revised upwards. Source: @fda2026artificial.
