# fig-07-01

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show readers that AI depends on a layered industrial stack, that each layer depends on the one below, and where the chokepoints are (layers dominated by one or very few firms).
- **caption:** Figure 7.1: The AI supply chain as a stack. Each layer depends on the one below, and several layers are dominated by one or a few firms (named examples are illustrative, not exhaustive). The marked chokepoints (extreme-ultraviolet lithography, leading-edge fabrication, and high-bandwidth memory with advanced packaging) are where governments have focused export controls. Diagram by the author, based on @sajadieh2026ai, @asml2026annual and @tsmc2026annual.

## content

Layout: bottom → top, six stacked horizontal bands of equal width (like a layer cake), with an upward arrow on the left side labelled "depends on ↓ / supplies ↑". Each band has a short label (bold, left) and example firms (right, smaller text). Mark chokepoint bands with a thicker border and a small padlock-style or "!" marker plus the word "chokepoint".

Bands (bottom to top):

1. **Chip-making tools** — examples: "ASML (EUV: sole maker); Zeiss (ASML's sole optics supplier)". CHOKEPOINT. [source: asml2026annual]
2. **Chip fabrication** — examples: "TSMC, Samsung". CHOKEPOINT (TSMC fabricates virtually every leading AI chip). [sajadieh2026ai]
3. **Memory & packaging** — examples: "SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron (HBM); TSMC (CoWoS), ASE, Amkor (packaging)". CHOKEPOINT (three HBM makers, limited packaging lines; §7.2 Concept box; the binding constraints of 2025 per IEA). [sajadieh2026ai; nvidia2026form; tsmc2026annual; iea2026key]
4. **Chip design** — examples: "Nvidia, AMD, Google (TPU), Huawei". [sajadieh2026ai; jouppi2017indatacenter]
5. **Cloud & data centres** — examples: "Hyperscalers (e.g. Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta); neo-clouds". Side note attached to this band: "needs electricity + water" with a small arrow from an "Energy & grid" box placed to the right of this band. [iea2026key]
6. **Models & applications** — examples: "Closed: API access | Open-weight: downloadable". Split this top band in two halves labelled "Closed-weight" and "Open-weight".

Right-hand annotation bracket spanning bands 1–4 labelled "Targets of export controls (2022–)".

Style: greyscale line diagram, labels ≤ 4 words in the main boxes; examples in smaller type.
