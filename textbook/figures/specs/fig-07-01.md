# fig-07-01

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show readers that AI depends on a layered industrial stack in which each layer supplies the one above, that two specialised inputs (design software and chip-making tools) and energy feed in from the side, and where the chokepoints are (stages dominated by one or very few firms).
- **caption:** Figure 7.1: The AI supply chain as a stack. Each layer supplies the one above; design software and chip-making tools feed in from the side, as does energy. Several stages are dominated by one or a few firms (named examples are illustrative, not exhaustive). Export controls since 2022 have targeted the advanced chips themselves and the marked chokepoints. Diagram by the author, based on @sajadieh2026ai, @asml2026annual, @tsmc2026annual and @bis2022eda.

## content

Layout: bottom → top, five stacked horizontal bands of equal width (like a layer cake) in the left two-thirds of the canvas, with an upward arrow on the far left labelled (rotated, no arrow glyphs in the text) "each layer supplies the one above". Each band has a short label (bold, left) and example firms (smaller text). Chokepoints have a thicker border and a small black "!" marker; a legend at top right reads "chokepoint: one or very few suppliers".

Main bands (bottom to top), in supply-chain order (design is upstream of fabrication and packaging):

1. **Chip design** — "Nvidia, AMD, Google (TPU), Huawei". [sajadieh2026ai; jouppi2017indatacenter]
2. **Chip fabrication** — "TSMC, Samsung". CHOKEPOINT (TSMC fabricates virtually every leading AI chip). [sajadieh2026ai]
3. **Memory & packaging** — "SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron (HBM); TSMC (CoWoS), ASE, Amkor (packaging)". CHOKEPOINT. [sajadieh2026ai; nvidia2026form; tsmc2026annual; iea2026key]
4. **Cloud & data centres** — "Hyperscalers (e.g. Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta); neo-clouds".
5. **Models & applications** — split in two halves "Closed-weight / API access" and "Open-weight / downloadable".

Side inputs (boxes on the right, each with an arrow into its band):

- Beside band 1: **Design software (EDA)** — "Synopsys, Cadence, Siemens EDA". CHOKEPOINT. [bis2022eda; cadence2025form]
- Beside band 2: **Chip-making tools** — "ASML (sole EUV maker); Zeiss (ASML's optics)". CHOKEPOINT. [asml2026annual]
- Beside band 4: **Energy & grid** — "electricity + water". [iea2026key]

No export-control bracket: the caption states that controls targeted the chips and the marked chokepoints (this reconciles the earlier bracket, which wrongly spanned chip design only).

Style: greyscale line diagram, labels ≤ 4 words in the main boxes; examples in smaller type.

Revision (Oct 2026, external review): arrow label no longer uses rotated ←/→ glyphs; chip design moved below fabrication and packaging; EDA added as a chokepoint; bracket replaced by caption wording.
