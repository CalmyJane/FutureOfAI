# fig-09-03

- **type:** bar chart (grouped, vertical)
- **purpose:** Show that Google search visits with an AI summary led to fewer clicks on traditional links and more ended sessions, which underlies publishers' concerns about search traffic.
- **caption:** Figure 9.3: Share of Google search visits in which users clicked a link, with and without an AI-generated summary on the page, and share of visits that ended the browsing session. Based on the browsing data of 900 US adults, March 2025. Source: @chapekis2025google.

## content

y-axis: "Share of search page visits (%)", linear, 0 to 30.
x-axis: three categories, each with up to two bars ("With AI summary" = dark grey; "Without AI summary" = light grey).

| Category | With AI summary | Without AI summary | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clicked a traditional search result link | 8% | 15% | chapekis2025google |
| Clicked a link inside the AI summary | 1% | (not applicable; leave blank, label "n/a") | chapekis2025google |
| Ended browsing session on this page | 26% | 16% | chapekis2025google |

Footnote text under chart: "18% of the 68,879 Google searches studied produced an AI summary."  (Source: chapekis2025google)
