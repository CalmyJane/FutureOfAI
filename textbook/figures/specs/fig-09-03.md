# fig-09-03

- **type:** bar chart (horizontal, grouped by study, with error bars where available)
- **purpose:** Show readers the size of AI persuasion effects found in recent large experiments, that conversation beats a static message, that effects in a high-salience US presidential race were smaller than in Canada, Poland or a ballot measure, and that these effects are large relative to the near-zero average effect of traditional campaign contact in US general elections.
- **caption:** Figure 9.3: Persuasive effects of AI in recent experiments, in points on 0–100 attitude or preference scales, with 95% confidence intervals where reported. Conversation with AI produced larger effects than a static AI-written message, and effects were smaller in the high-salience US presidential race than in Canada, Poland or a state ballot measure. The studies measure effects differently (treatment versus control, or the gap between two opposing AI advocates), so bars are comparable only within each study. Source: @hackenburg2025levers; @lin2025persuading; @kalla2018minimal.
