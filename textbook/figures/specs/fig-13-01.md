# fig-13-01

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show the four risk tiers of the EU AI Act, what each tier means in practice, and that general-purpose AI models are regulated in a separate track that cuts across the tiers.
- **caption:** Figure 13.1: The risk tiers of the EU AI Act. Obligations rise towards the top of the pyramid; general-purpose AI models are regulated in a separate track that cuts across the tiers, with extra duties for models presumed to pose "systemic risk". The tiers can overlap: a high-risk system may also carry transparency duties. Source: [@europeanparliament2024regulation; @europeanparliament2026regulation]

## Content
Layout: a four-level pyramid on the left (about two-thirds of the width); a vertical two-box column on the right labelled "General-purpose AI models", joined to the pyramid by a dashed bracket spanning all levels, its connector meeting the gap between the two GPAI boxes.

Pyramid levels (top to bottom), each with a bold tier label and a smaller example line:
1. Top (smallest): **Unacceptable risk: banned** — examples: "Social scoring · face scraping · emotion at work". Small note (two lines): "Art. 5; since Feb 2025" / "+ deepfake, CSAM bans Dec 2026" (new Art. 5(1)(ba)–(bb) inserted by Reg. 2026/1744, applying from 2 Dec 2026).
2. **High risk: strict requirements** — examples: "Hiring, credit, education", "Product safety components". Small note: "Annex III: Dec 2027; Annex I: Aug 2028" (Art. 113 as amended by Reg. 2026/1744).
3. **Transparency risk: disclosure** — examples: "Chatbots", "Deepfakes", "AI-generated content". Small note: "Art. 50; from Aug 2026".
4. Bottom (widest): **Minimal risk: no new duties** — examples: "Spam filters", "Video games".

Right column, two stacked boxes:
- Upper box: **GPAI models** — "Documentation", "Copyright policy", "Training-data summary". Note: "Art. 53; since Aug 2025".
- Lower box (heavier border): **GPAI with systemic risk** — "> 10^25 FLOP (presumed)", "Evaluations, incident reports". Note: "Arts 51, 55; since Aug 2025" (Art. 51(2) presumption; Art. 55 duties).

Arrow on far left pointing upward labelled "Obligations increase".
Footnote below the pyramid, small italic: "Tiers can overlap: a high-risk system may also carry transparency duties." (Art. 50 applies in addition to Arts 8–15; the pyramid is the Commission's communication device, not an exclusive legal classification.)
Greyscale: shade levels from dark (top) to light (bottom).
