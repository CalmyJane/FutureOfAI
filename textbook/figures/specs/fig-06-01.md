# fig-06-01

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show the reader that AI enters every stage of the research cycle, that it does a different job at each stage (predict, emulate, search, automate), and that each output needs a matching check. The inner ring of checks is referred to again in Section 6.8.
- **caption:** Figure 6.1: Where AI enters the scientific cycle. Each stage of a simplified research cycle is paired with the kind of AI tool most often used there, and with the check that keeps the result honest. The checks on the inner ring return in Section 6.8. Diagram by the author.
- **content:**
  - Layout: a clockwise circle of six stage boxes (outer ring), each with a small tag beside it naming the AI job; a concentric inner ring of six short "check" labels aligned with each stage. Centre label: "Scientific cycle".
  - Outer ring, stage boxes (clockwise from top), with AI-job tag in brackets:
    1. "Read literature" [tag: "Automate: search, summarise"]
    2. "Form hypothesis" [tag: "Search: propose candidates"]
    3. "Design experiment / simulation" [tag: "Emulate: fast surrogate"]
    4. "Collect data" [tag: "Predict: fill gaps"]
    5. "Analyse data" [tag: "Automate: write code"]
    6. "Publish and review" [tag: "Automate: draft, review"]
  - Arrows: clockwise arrows from each stage box to the next; arrow from 6 back to 1.
  - Inner ring check labels (aligned with the matching stage):
    1. "Check sources"
    2. "Expert judgement"
    3. "Compare with reality"
    4. "Test on held-out data"
    5. "Read the code"
    6. "Independent replication"
  - A small legend at bottom: "Outer tags = AI job; inner ring = verification".
  - Short labels only (≤ 4 words); greyscale; no logos.
