# Figure 3.1 — Tokenisation of a sentence

- **type:** table-figure (two panels)
- **purpose:** Show students exactly what a tokeniser does to a real sentence (words vs. sub-word pieces, leading spaces, ID numbers), and that the same content costs different numbers of tokens in different languages.
- **caption:** Figure 3.1: A real tokenisation. The sentence "Students in Edinburgh rarely misunderstand tokenisation." becomes eight tokens, each with an ID number, in the o200k_base tokeniser used for GPT-4o; the lower panel shows that the same short sentence needs more tokens in some languages than in others. Token counts computed by the author with tiktoken 0.14.0 on 22 October 2026. Source: [@openai2026tiktoken; @petrov2023language]
- **content:**
  - **Panel A (top), left→right row of 8 boxes.** Above the row, the full sentence in plain text. Each box shows the token text (use a visible marker such as "␣" for a leading space) and, beneath it in smaller type, the token ID. Brace under boxes 6–7 labelled "one word, two tokens".
    | # | token text | ID (o200k_base) |
    |---|---|---|
    | 1 | `Students` | 36954 |
    | 2 | `␣in` | 306 |
    | 3 | `␣Edinburgh` | 61877 |
    | 4 | `␣rarely` | 32208 |
    | 5 | `␣misunderstand` | 169786 |
    | 6 | `␣token` | 6602 |
    | 7 | `isation` | 6993 |
    | 8 | `.` | 13 |
    Footer line in panel A: "6 words → 8 tokens".
  - **Panel B (bottom): small table or horizontal bar chart (linear scale, x-axis "Tokens", 0–65).** Sentence: "I love learning about language models." and translations. Two bars per language: o200k_base (GPT-4o era, 2024) and GPT-2 tokeniser (2019).
    | Language | Text used | o200k_base tokens | GPT-2 tokens |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | English | I love learning about language models. | 7 | 7 |
    | German | Ich lerne gern über Sprachmodelle. | 9 | 15 |
    | Hindi | मुझे भाषा मॉडल के बारे में सीखना पसंद है। | 12 | 62 |
    | Swahili | Ninapenda kujifunza kuhusu mifano ya lugha. | 13 | 20 |
    All counts computed by the author with tiktoken 0.14.0 (encodings `o200k_base` and `gpt2`), 22 Oct 2026 [@openai2026tiktoken]. Optional note in panel: "Same text can differ up to 15× in length across languages [@petrov2023language]."
  - Greyscale: o200k bars dark grey, GPT-2 bars light grey with hatch.
