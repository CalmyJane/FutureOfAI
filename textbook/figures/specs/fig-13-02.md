# fig-13-02

- **type:** table-figure
- **purpose:** Show precisely which EU AI Act application dates the 2026 Digital Omnibus changed and which it left unchanged.
- **caption:** Figure 13.2: Key application dates of the EU AI Act before and after the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744). The amendment left the dates for prohibitions and general-purpose AI unchanged but deferred the high-risk obligations by 12 to 16 months. Source: [@europeanparliament2024regulation; @europeanparliament2026regulation]

## Content
Three-column table. Columns: "Provision" | "Original date (Reg. 2024/1689)" | "After omnibus (Reg. 2026/1744)". Rows (highlight changed cells with a darker grey fill):

| Provision | Original date | After omnibus |
|---|---|---|
| Entry into force | 1 Aug 2024 | unchanged |
| Prohibited practices (Art. 5) | 2 Feb 2025 | unchanged |
| New bans: intimate deepfakes, CSAM | — | 2 Dec 2026 (new) |
| GPAI model obligations (Arts 53, 55) | 2 Aug 2025 | unchanged |
| Commission fines on GPAI providers (Art. 101) | 2 Aug 2026 | unchanged |
| GPAI models placed on market before Aug 2025 | 2 Aug 2027 | unchanged |
| Transparency duties (Art. 50) | 2 Aug 2026 | unchanged; machine-readable marking for systems already on market by 2 Dec 2026 |
| High-risk, Annex III (e.g. hiring, credit, education) | 2 Aug 2026 | 2 Dec 2027 |
| High-risk, Annex I (product safety components) | 2 Aug 2027 | 2 Aug 2028 |

Sources: original dates from Art. 113 and Art. 111(3) of Reg. 2024/1689 [@europeanparliament2024regulation]; amended dates from Art. 113 as amended and Art. 111(4) and new Art. 5(1)(ba)-(bb) [@europeanparliament2026regulation; @councileu2026proposal].
Note: the deferral is 16 months for Annex III (2 Aug 2026 → 2 Dec 2027) and 12 months for Annex I (2 Aug 2027 → 2 Aug 2028).
