# Figure 3.2 — Transformer / attention schematic

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show the path of text through a GPT-style (decoder-only) transformer (tokens → embeddings + position → repeated blocks of attention and feed-forward → next-token probabilities), and give one concrete picture of what attention does.
- **caption:** Figure 3.2: A GPT-style transformer, simplified. Tokens become embeddings plus position information (learned position embeddings, as in GPT-2), and each token's vector then runs up its own residual stream (vertical lines) through many identical blocks. In each block an attention step brings in information from the token itself and earlier tokens only (the causal mask), and a feed-forward step processes each token on its own; both add their results to the stream (⊕) rather than replacing it. Layer normalisation is omitted. The final layer gives a probability for every possible next token; the bars show GPT-2 XL's four most likely next tokens after "The cat sat on the". Inset: two attention heads in layer 5 of a trained translation model attend from "its" to "Law" and "application"; this part of that model is not masked, so it can also look ahead; line weights are qualitative. Diagram by the author, after @vaswani2017attention; probabilities computed by the author with GPT-2 XL. Source: [@vaswani2017attention; @radford2019language]
- **content (main diagram, bottom→top):**
  1. Bottom row: token boxes "The", "␣cat", "␣sat", "␣on", "␣the".
  2. Arrow up into a layer of boxes "Embedding" with a "+" merging a side box "Position info".
  3. A large rounded rectangle labelled "Transformer block" containing two stacked boxes: "Masked attention" (lower; draw thin curved lines between token columns, only pointing backwards/leftwards, label "itself and earlier only" (revised 22 Oct 2026: with the causal mask a token also attends to itself; previous label "looks only left")) and "Feed-forward" (upper; one small box per token column, no connections between columns). A side bracket "× N blocks (e.g. 96 in GPT-3)".
  4. Arrow up to box "Output layer".
  5. Top: small horizontal bar list "Next-token probabilities" with real values from GPT-2 XL for the prompt "The cat sat on the" (computed by the author, 22 Oct 2026, temperature 1; model source [@radford2019language]): "␣edge" 7.3%, "␣floor" 5.2%, "␣table" 4.9%, "␣bed" 4.4%, then "… 50,000+ other tokens". Linear scale 0–10%.
- **Inset (right side):** the sentence "The Law will never be perfect , but its application should be just" written horizontally; thick lines from "its" to "Law" and to "application"; thin lines to other words. Inset label: "Attention from 'its' (layer 5 of 6)". Inset source note: [@vaswani2017attention, Figure 4]. Line thickness qualitative only.
- Layout: main diagram bottom→top on left two-thirds; inset on right third; greyscale.
