# fig-13-03

- **type:** timeline
- **purpose:** Let students see how national and regional laws and international initiatives unfolded in parallel from 2023 to October 2026, and that binding rules came mainly from national/regional levels.
- **caption:** Figure 13.3: Selected milestones in AI governance, 2023–2026, by jurisdiction. National and regional laws (upper lanes) developed alongside non-binding international declarations and one binding treaty (lower lanes). Source: [@ukgov2023bletchley; @dsit2024frontier; @elysee2025statement; @pib2026india; @councilofeurope2024framework; @un2025terms; @europeanparliament2024regulation; @europeanparliament2026regulation; @biden2023safe; @trump2025ensuring; @cac2025measures; @californiagovernor2025governor]
