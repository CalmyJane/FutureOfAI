# tbl-06-01

- **type:** table (pipe table in chapters/ch06.md; no drawing needed)
- **purpose:** Let the reader compare, in one place, what the developers of five machine-learning weather systems reported, against which baseline, and at what computing cost, and see the reported weaknesses next to the strengths.
- **caption:** Table 6.1: Published results for five machine-learning weather systems, as reported by their developers. The table lists the comparison baseline, the share of targets on which the AI model was more skilful (where reported), the reported computing savings and a reported weakness. All figures are developers' own evaluations; targets and baselines differ between rows, so rows are not directly comparable. Source: [@lam2023learning; @price2025probabilistic; @ecmwf2025ecmwfs; @ecmwf2025ensemble; @noaa2025noaa; @nws2026aigfs].
- **content:** As set in the chapter: five columns, System (with type and date in brackets) | Compared with | Reported skill | Reported cost / speed | Reported weakness. Exact values (the Type and date and Source key columns below are folded into the System cell and the caption):

| System | Type and date | Compared with | Reported skill | Reported cost / speed | Reported weakness | Source key |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GraphCast | Deterministic; research paper, *Science*, 2023 | ECMWF HRES (deterministic) | Better on 90% of 1,380 targets | 10-day forecast in under 1 minute on one TPU v4 (HRES: about 1 hour on a supercomputer) | Blurring at long lead times | lam2023learning |
| GenCast | 50-member ensemble; research paper, *Nature*, 2024/2025 | ECMWF ENS (ensemble) | Better on 97.2% of 1,320 targets | One 15-day forecast in about 8 minutes on one TPU v5 | Higher cost than deterministic ML models | price2025probabilistic |
| AIFS (ECMWF) | Deterministic (AIFS Single); operational since 25 Feb 2025 | Physics-based models | Better on many measures, incl. tropical cyclone tracks (gains of up to 20%) | About 1,000 times less energy per forecast | Uses physics-based initial conditions | ecmwf2025ecmwfs |
| AIFS ENS (ECMWF) | 51-member ensemble; operational since 1 Jul 2025 | Physics-based models | Better on many measures, incl. surface temperature (gains of up to 20%) | Over 10 times faster; about 1,000 times less energy | Coarser grid than physics-based ensemble (31 km vs 9 km) | ecmwf2025ensemble |
| AIGFS / AIGEFS (NOAA) | Operational suite launched 17 Dec 2025; GraphCast as starting point, fine-tuned on NOAA analyses | GFS / GEFS | Improved skill for many large-scale features; ensemble adds 18–24 h of skill | Up to 99.7% less computing (AIGFS); 9% of GEFS computing (AIGEFS) | Worse tropical cyclone intensity forecasts (v1.0; July 2026 upgrade reported to improve them) | noaa2025noaa; nws2026aigfs |

  - Footnote row under the table: "All figures are developers' own evaluations; targets and baselines differ between rows, so rows are not directly comparable."
