# fig-07-02

- **type:** timeline
- **purpose:** Let readers see the sequence and changing direction of US export controls on AI chips, 2022–2026, at a glance.
- **caption:** Figure 7.2: Main US export-control measures on advanced AI chips and related items, October 2022 to July 2026. The direction of policy changed in 2025. Rules continued to restrict China, while the global licensing framework of January 2025 was abandoned before it took full effect; the promised replacement had not been issued by October 2026, and access was handled through country-specific measures such as the July 2026 UAE rule. Source: bis2022implementation; bis2023implementation; bis2024foreignproduced; bis2025framework; bis2025department; nvidia2025form; bis2025expansion; bis2025one; bis2026revision; bis2026enhanced.

## content

Layout: horizontal timeline, left → right, from Oct 2022 to Jul 2026, with a single axis line and events alternating above/below. Use marker shape to code direction: filled square = tightening; open circle = loosening/withdrawal; diamond = partner access.

Events (date — short label (≤ 4 words) — one-line note — source key):

1. 7 Oct 2022 — "Advanced chip controls" — licences for advanced AI chips and tools to China — bis2022implementation (87 FR 62186; effective 7 Oct 2022, published 13 Oct 2022). Marker: tightening.
2. 17 Oct 2023 (announced; published 25 Oct 2023) — "Thresholds revised" — adds performance density; A800/H800 covered — bis2023implementation (88 FR 73458); nvidia2023form. Marker: tightening.
3. 2 Dec 2024 (published 5 Dec 2024) — "HBM and tools" — controls on high-bandwidth memory, more equipment — bis2024foreignproduced (89 FR 96790). Marker: tightening.
4. 13 Jan 2025 (effective; published 15 Jan 2025; compliance date 15 May 2025) — "AI Diffusion Framework" — global tiered licensing; closed-weight model controls — bis2025framework (90 FR 4544). Marker: tightening.
5. 9 Apr 2025 — "H20 licence required" — Nvidia told licence needed for China — nvidia2025form. Marker: tightening.
6. 13 May 2025 — "Rescission announced" — diffusion rule not enforced; replacement promised — bis2025department. Marker: loosening. (Fact-check note: BIS announced the rescission and stopped enforcement; the July 2026 UAE rule shows the worldwide licence requirement still in the EAR but enforced only for certain destinations, so do not label this a completed rescission.)
6a. 29 Sep 2025 (effective; published 30 Sep 2025) and 10 Nov 2025 (suspension effective; published 12 Nov 2025; stayed until 9 Nov 2026) — one combined label "Affiliates rule, then stayed" with date "29 Sep / 10 Nov 2025" — "50%-owned affiliates of listed entities covered; stayed for a year" — bis2025expansion (90 FR 47201); bis2025one (90 FR 50857). Two markers: tightening square at 29 Sep, open circle at 10 Nov joined to the label stem by a short bracket.
7. 15 Jan 2026 — "H200 case-by-case" — China review moved from denial to case-by-case — bis2026revision (91 FR 1684). Marker: loosening.
8. 10 Jul 2026 (effective; published 14 Jul 2026) — "UAE licence-free access" — partner access to advanced computing items — bis2026enhanced (91 FR 43034). Marker: partner access.

Legend: ■ tightening · ○ loosening · ◆ partner access.

Footnote under legend: "Effective dates, except 17 Oct 2023 and 13 May 2025 (announcements) and 9 Apr 2025 (notice to Nvidia)." (88 FR 73458 was announced 17 Oct, published 25 Oct, effective 17 Nov 2023.)

Deliberately omitted to keep the timeline legible: Nvidia's Aug 2025 H20 licences and Feb 2026 H200 licence (individual licences, not rules) and allied Dutch/EU controls (not US measures); all are in the §7.3 text.

_Revision after external review (Oct 2026): caption synced with the chapter (replacement rule never issued; country-specific measures); no data change._
