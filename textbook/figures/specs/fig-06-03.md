# fig-06-03

- **type:** timeline
- **purpose:** Show the pace and the changing nature of AI results in mathematics and algorithms between 2023 and 2026: from narrow search and formal systems to natural-language olympiad proofs and AI-generated proofs of open problems, and make clear which results were independently checked.
- **caption:** Figure 6.3: Selected milestones in AI for mathematics and algorithms, 2023–2026. Entries are limited to results with a published paper, an official announcement, or a verified formal proof. Source: [@romeraparedes2024mathematical; @trinh2024solving; @google2024ai; @mankowitz2023faster; @novikov2025alphaevolve; @google2025advanced; @georgiev2025mathematical; @sothanaphan2026resolution].
- **content:**
  - Layout: horizontal timeline, left→right, from June 2023 to January 2026; entries alternate above and below the axis. Each entry: date, system name, ≤ 12-word description. A small marker distinguishes "peer-reviewed paper" (filled circle), "company announcement / preprint" (open circle) and "Lean-verified proof" (square).
  - Entries:
    1. Jun 2023 — AlphaDev — "Faster sorting routines added to LLVM C++ library" — filled circle — mankowitz2023faster
    2. Dec 2023 — FunSearch — "Larger cap sets than previously known" — filled circle — romeraparedes2024mathematical (published online 14 Dec 2023)
    3. Jan 2024 — AlphaGeometry — "25 of 30 olympiad geometry problems" — filled circle — trinh2024solving
    4. Jul 2024 — AlphaProof + AlphaGeometry 2 — "IMO 28/42 (silver level); up to 3 days' computing" — open circle — google2024ai (peer-reviewed later as hubert2025olympiad)

    6. Jul 2025 — Gemini Deep Think — "IMO 35/42 (gold); natural language; certified" — open circle — google2025advanced
