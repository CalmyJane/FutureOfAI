# fig-09-01

- **type:** line chart (dual y-axis)
- **purpose:** Show that a text watermark survives light editing, and that removing it by rewriting with a weaker model costs a large loss of fluency: detection quality falls only as perplexity rises steeply.
- **caption:** Figure 9.1: Trade-off between removing a text watermark and preserving text quality. A smaller model (T5-Large) replaced words in 500 watermarked passages of about 200 tokens, up to a growing share of the tokens. Detection quality (AUC, left axis; 0.5 = chance) falls as more words are replaced, while perplexity (right axis; higher = less fluent text) rises from 6.3 to 33.9. At the paper's detection threshold (z = 4), the share of watermarked passages still flagged falls faster than the AUC suggests: from 98.4% unattacked to 82% at ε = 0.1 and 35% at ε = 0.3 (the paper's Table 8). Watermark settings γ = 0.5, δ = 2.0, multinomial sampling. Source: @kirchenbauer2023watermark.

## content

x-axis: "Share of tokens replaced by the attacker (ε)", linear, 0 to 0.7, ticks at 0, 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7.

Left y-axis: "Detection AUC", linear, 0.5 to 1.0; the axis starts at chance level, noted in the plot as "AUC 0.5 = chance" (a separate dashed line at 0.5 would coincide with the x-axis).
Right y-axis: "Perplexity (lower = more fluent)", linear, 0 to 50 (0 to 40 made the two series overlap at ε = 0.5).

Data (all from Kirchenbauer et al. 2023, Figure 6 legend; the AUC and PPL values are printed as numbers in the legend of the source figure and in Section 7.1, not read off the plotted curves; T5 span attack on γ = 0.5, δ = 2.0 soft watermark, multinomial sampling, T = 200 ± 5 tokens, 500 sequences) [kirchenbauer2023watermark]:

| ε (share replaced) | AUC | Perplexity |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 (unattacked) | 0.998 | 6.3 |
| 0.1 | 0.988 | 13.1 |
| 0.3 | 0.954 | 21.2 |
| 0.5 | 0.838 | 28.4 |
| 0.7 | 0.696 | 33.9 |

Series: AUC as solid black line with circle markers (left axis); perplexity as dashed grey line with square markers (right axis). Label each point with its value in small type, placed on the side away from both lines. Legend above the plot.

Style: greyscale; no fill.

Note (Oct 2026 revision): true-positive rates at z = 4 after attack, multinomial sampling, from Kirchenbauer et al. Table 8: ε 0.1 → 0.819; 0.3 → 0.353; 0.5 → 0.094; 0.7 → 0.039 (unattacked 0.984). Given in the caption, not plotted.
