# fig-12-03

- **type:** bar chart (grouped)
- **purpose:** Show how much more a monitor detects when it can read a reasoning model's chain of thought than when it sees only the model's actions, using the two systemic reward hacks reported in Baker et al. (2025).
- **caption:** Figure 12.3: Share of two systemic reward hacks detected by a GPT-4o-based monitor that read only the agent's actions or also its chain of thought, during reinforcement-learning training of an OpenAI frontier reasoning model. Source: [@baker2025monitoring].
- **content:**
  - Layout: grouped vertical bars; three groups on the x-axis, two bars per group.
  - x-axis groups (labels): "exit(0) hack", "SkipTest hack", "Both hacks".
  - Bars per group: "Actions only" (light grey) and "Chain of thought + actions" (dark grey).
  - y-axis: "Recall (% of hacks detected)", linear, 0 to 100, gridlines at 20.
  - Data (recall, per cent), all from [@baker2025monitoring], Table 1:
    - exit(0) hack: Actions only 40; Chain of thought + actions 93
    - SkipTest hack: Actions only 80; Chain of thought + actions 97
    - Both hacks (joint): Actions only 60; Chain of thought + actions 95
  - Value labels on top of each bar (e.g. "95%").
  - Small note under the chart: "Approximate precision (joint): actions only 81%, chain of thought 77%." (same source, Table 1)
  - Legend top left. Greyscale only.
  - Verified 2026-10-23 against arXiv:2503.11926 Table 1 ("Action Monitor" = actions, tool calls and outputs; "CoT Monitor" = CoT, actions and output; recall and approximate precision measured against a regex detector over all of training).
