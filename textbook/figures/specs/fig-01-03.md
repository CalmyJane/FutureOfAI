# fig-01-03

- **type:** timeline
- **purpose:** Give readers one visual overview of AI's history from 1950 to 2026, showing the alternation of optimism and AI winters, the shift from symbolic AI to machine learning and deep learning, and the recent acceleration of deployment and policy events.
- **caption:** Figure 1.3: Selected milestones in the history of AI, 1950–2026. Shaded bands mark the two AI winters, whose start and end dates are approximate. Research milestones are to the left of the axis, deployment and policy events to the right; the axis is compressed before 2005. Sources: @turing1950computing; @mccarthy2006proposal; @samuel1959studies; @lighthill1973artificial; @yu1979antimicrobial; @mcdermott1985dark; @mitchell2021why; @rumelhart1986learning; @brown1990statistical; @ibm2025deep; @deng2009imagenet; @seide2011conversational; @krizhevsky2012imagenet; @silver2016mastering; @vaswani2017attention; @devlin2019bert; @brown2020language; @bommasani2021opportunities; @chatterji2025how; @oecd2024explanatory; @europeanparliament2024regulation; @openai2024o1; @anthropic2024introducing; @guo2025deepseek; @malik2026chatgpt.
- **content:**
  - Horizontal axis 1950 → 2026, linear in years, but with a visual break or compressed scale 1950–2005 and expanded scale 2009–2026 (note "axis compressed before 2005" in small text), because most recent events cluster after 2012.
  - Shaded bands (light grey, hatched), labelled:
    - "First AI winter" ≈ 1974–1980 (approximate; Mitchell 2021 places it after the 1973 reports and before the early-1980s upturn) — source: mitchell2021why
    - "Second AI winter" ≈ 1987–1993 (approximate; Mitchell 2021: "the late 1980's marked the beginning of a new AI winter", and "about five years after" McDermott's 1984 warning; widely used summary accounts date it from 1987, and its end date varies by account, from the early 1990s to as late as 2000, so draw the right edge of this band faded/open-ended rather than as a hard line) — source: mitchell2021why
  - Era labels along the top (thin brackets): "Symbolic AI" 1956–late 1980s; "Statistical ML" 1990s–2000s; "Deep learning" 2012→; "LLMs and agents" 2020→ — source: mitchell2021why (eras), chapter text.
