# fig-01-01

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show readers how the main AI terms relate: AI ⊃ machine learning ⊃ deep learning ⊃ generative foundation models / LLMs, with symbolic AI as a separate region inside AI and "agents" as a cross-cutting usage pattern. The reader should also see that the nesting is approximate.
- **caption:** Figure 1.1: The usual nesting of AI terms. Machine learning is one approach within AI; deep learning is one kind of machine learning; large language models and other generative foundation models are, today, mostly built with deep learning. Agents are drawn across the boundaries because the term describes how a model is used. Schematic, not to scale. Diagram by the author.
- **content:**
  - Four nested rounded shapes (ellipses or rounded rectangles), outermost to innermost:
    1. Outer: label "Artificial intelligence"
    2. Inside it, occupying the right two-thirds: "Machine learning"
    3. Inside ML: "Deep learning"
    4. Inside DL: "Foundation models", and within or overlapping it a smaller shape "LLMs" plus a sibling small shape "Image/audio generators"
  - In the left third of the outer shape (outside the ML shape), a separate box: "Symbolic AI" with a small sub-label "Expert systems".
  - Inside the ML shape but outside DL, small example tag: "Support vector machines".
  - A dashed bracket or translucent horizontal band labelled "Agents (model + tools + loop)" crossing from the Foundation-models/LLMs region outward across the DL and ML boundaries, to show it is a usage pattern, not a nested layer.
  - A small dotted shape labelled "Generative AI" overlapping the foundation-model region and extending partly outside it (to signal that not all generative models are foundation models, and not all foundation models are generative).
  - Optional tiny example labels (≤ 4 words each): "MYCIN (1970s)" in Symbolic AI; "AlexNet (2012)" in DL; "GPT-3 (2020)" in LLMs.
  - Layout: left→right; outermost shape fills width; greyscale with hatching to distinguish nested levels. No arrows needed.
