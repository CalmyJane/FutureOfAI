# fig-09-02

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show how content provenance (C2PA Content Credentials) travels with a file from creation to display, how editing adds signed entries, how a watermark (soft binding) can recover a stripped manifest, and that provenance is not a truth check.
- **caption:** Figure 9.2: A simplified content provenance pipeline. A camera or AI tool creates content and a signed manifest; each editing step adds a new signed entry; a platform or viewer checks the signatures against the file's hash. If the manifest is stripped, a watermark (soft binding) can help retrieve it from cloud storage. Provenance shows where content came from, not whether it is true. Diagram by the author, based on @c2pa2025c2pa.
