# fig-06-02

- **type:** table-figure
- **purpose:** Let students compare, in one place, what the developers of four machine-learning weather systems reported, against which baseline, and at what computing cost, and see the reported weaknesses next to the strengths.
- **caption:** Figure 6.2: Published results for four machine-learning weather systems, as reported by their developers. The table lists the comparison baseline, the share of targets on which the AI model was more skilful (where reported), and the reported computing savings. Source: [@lam2023learning; @price2025probabilistic; @ecmwf2025ecmwfs; @noaa2025noaa].
- **content:** A table with five columns: System | Type and date | Compared with | Reported skill | Reported cost / speed. Add a final narrow column "Reported weakness" where noted. Exact values:

| System | Type and date | Compared with | Reported skill | Reported cost / speed | Reported weakness | Source key |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GraphCast | Deterministic; research paper, *Science*, 2023 | ECMWF HRES (deterministic) | Better on 90% of 1,380 targets | 10-day forecast in under 1 minute on one TPU v4 (HRES: about 1 hour on a supercomputer) | Blurring at long lead times | lam2023learning |
| GenCast | 50-member ensemble; research paper, *Nature*, 2024/2025 | ECMWF ENS (ensemble) | Better on 97.2% of 1,320 targets | One 15-day forecast in about 8 minutes on one TPU v5 | Higher cost than deterministic ML models | price2025probabilistic |
| AIFS (ECMWF) | Operational since 25 Feb 2025 | Physics-based models | Up to 20% gain for tropical cyclone tracks | About 1,000 times less energy per forecast | Uses physics-based initial conditions | ecmwf2025ecmwfs |
| AIGFS / AIGEFS (NOAA) | Operational since 17 Dec 2025; built from GraphCast | GFS / GEFS | Improved skill for many large-scale features; ensemble adds 18–24 h of skill | Up to 99.7% less computing (AIGFS); 9% of GEFS computing (AIGEFS) | Worse tropical cyclone intensity forecasts (v1.0) | noaa2025noaa |

  - Footnote row under the table: "All figures are developers' own evaluations; targets and baselines differ between rows, so rows are not directly comparable."
  - Notes for the drawer: "Blurring at long lead times" is from lam2023learning (Section on blurring); "Higher cost than deterministic ML models" is from price2025probabilistic (diffusion models need multiple evaluations per step); "Uses physics-based initial conditions" is from ecmwf2025ecmwfs.
