# Figure 3.3 — Transformer / attention schematic

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show the path of text through a decoder-only transformer (tokens → embeddings + position → repeated blocks of attention and feed-forward → next-token probabilities), and give one concrete picture of what attention does.
- **caption:** Figure 3.3: A decoder-only transformer, simplified. Tokens become embeddings plus position information, then pass through many identical blocks, each with an attention step (tokens exchange information) and a feed-forward step (each token is processed on its own). The final layer outputs next-token probabilities. Inset: attention from "its" to "Law" in a trained model. Diagram by the author, after @vaswani2017attention; probabilities computed by the author with GPT-2 XL. Source: [@vaswani2017attention; @radford2019language]
- **content (main diagram, bottom→top):**
  1. Bottom row: token boxes "The", "␣cat", "␣sat", "␣on", "␣the".
  2. Arrow up into a layer of boxes "Embedding" with a "+" merging a side box "Position info".
  3. A large rounded rectangle labelled "Transformer block" containing two stacked boxes: "Masked attention" (lower; draw thin curved lines between token columns, only pointing backwards/leftwards, label "looks only left") and "Feed-forward" (upper; one small box per token column, no connections between columns). A side bracket "× N blocks (e.g. 96 in GPT-3)".
  4. Arrow up to box "Output layer".
  5. Top: small horizontal bar list "Next-token probabilities" with real values from GPT-2 XL for the prompt "The cat sat on the" (computed by the author, 22 Oct 2026, temperature 1; model source [@radford2019language]): "␣edge" 7.3%, "␣floor" 5.2%, "␣table" 4.9%, "␣bed" 4.4%, then "… 50,000+ other tokens". Linear scale 0–10%.
- **Inset (right side):** the sentence "The Law will never be perfect , but its application should be just" written horizontally; thick lines from "its" to "Law" and to "application"; thin lines to other words. Inset label: "Attention from 'its' (layer 5 of 6)". Inset source note: [@vaswani2017attention, Figure 4]. Line thickness qualitative only.
- Layout: main diagram bottom→top on left two-thirds; inset on right third; greyscale.
