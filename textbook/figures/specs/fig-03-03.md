# Figure 3.3 — The training pipeline

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show the stages that turn raw text into a chat assistant and a reasoning model, with what data each stage uses and what it produces.
- **caption:** Figure 3.3: How a typical chat model is trained. Pre-training on web-scale text produces a base model that continues text. Training on written examples of good answers (instruction tuning) teaches it to follow instructions. Training on rankings of answers by people or by another AI (preference training) shapes its style and safety. Reasoning models add a further stage of reinforcement learning on problems whose answers can be checked automatically; in recent models this stage can use a substantial share of the training computation. Diagram by the author, based on [@ouyang2022training; @bai2022constitutional; @guo2025deepseekr1]
- **content (left→right main row, four stage boxes with arrows):**
  1. Input cylinder (far left) "Web-scale text" (sub-label "trillions of tokens") → stage box **"Pre-training"** (sub-label "predict next token") → output pill **"Base model"** (sub-label "continues text").
  2. Input above: "Written demonstrations" → stage box **"Instruction tuning"** (sub-label "supervised fine-tuning") → pill **"Instruction model"**.
  3. Input above: two small boxes "Human rankings (RLHF)" and "Principles + AI feedback (RLAIF)" feeding a small box "Reward model" → stage box **"Preference training"** (sub-label "RL or DPO") → pill **"Chat assistant"**.
  4. Branch (dashed arrow down-right from "Chat assistant" or from "Base model"): input "Problems with checkable answers" → stage box **"Reasoning RL"** (sub-label "reward correct answers") → pill **"Reasoning model"**.
- Under the row, a thin timeline-like annotation: width of the "Pre-training" box visibly larger, labelled "most compute"; the brace "far less data" should span only "Instruction tuning" and "Preference training"; give "Reasoning RL" its own label "growing share of compute" (revised 22 Oct 2026; Epoch AI estimate that DeepSeek-R1 RL ≈ 20% of base-model pre-training cost, @you2025how, cited in ch03 text). (Qualitative; no numbers.)
- Layout: left→right, greyscale, short labels.
