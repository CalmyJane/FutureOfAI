# Figure 3.3 — The training pipeline

- **type:** diagram
- **purpose:** Show the stages that turn raw text into a chat assistant and a reasoning model, with what data each stage uses and what it produces.
- **caption:** Figure 3.3: How a typical chat model is trained. Pre-training on web-scale text produces a base model that continues text. Training on written examples of good answers (instruction tuning) teaches it to follow instructions. Training on rankings of answers by people or by another AI (preference training) shapes its style and safety. Reasoning models add a further stage of reinforcement learning on problems whose answers can be checked automatically. This stage still uses far less computation than pre-training, about 5% for DeepSeek-R1, but its share is growing. Schematic by the author, based on @ouyang2022training, @bai2022constitutional and @guo2025deepseek; share of computation from @guo2025deepseek and @deepseekai2024deepseekv3.
- **content (left→right main row, four stage boxes with arrows):**
  1. Input cylinder (far left) "Web-scale text" (sub-label "trillions of tokens") → stage box **"Pre-training"** (sub-label "predict next token") → output pill **"Base model"** (sub-label "continues text").
  2. Input above: "Written demonstrations" → stage box **"Instruction tuning"** (sub-label "supervised fine-tuning") → pill **"Instruction model"**.
  3. Input above: two small boxes "Human rankings (RLHF)" and "Principles + AI feedback (RLAIF)" feeding a small box "Reward model" → stage box **"Preference training"** (sub-label "RL or DPO") → pill **"Chat assistant"**.
  4. Branch (dashed arrow down-right from "Chat assistant" or from "Base model"): input "Problems with checkable answers" → stage box **"Reasoning RL"** (sub-label "reward correct answers") → pill **"Reasoning model"**.
- Under the row, a thin timeline-like annotation: width of the "Pre-training" box visibly larger, labelled "most compute"; the brace "far less data" should span only "Instruction tuning" and "Preference training"; give "Reasoning RL" its own label "small but growing share" (revised 25 Oct 2026: DeepSeek's Nature SI reports ≈147K GPU-hours for the R1-Zero and R1 RL stages plus SFT data, ≈5% of DeepSeek-V3's 2.79M GPU-hours; the earlier Epoch AI estimate of ≈20%, @you2025how, is superseded). (Qualitative; no numbers.)
- Layout: left→right, greyscale, short labels.
