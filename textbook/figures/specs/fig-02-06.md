# Figure 2.6: Two views of generalisation (overfitting and double descent)

- **type:** line chart (schematic, conceptual), two panels side by side
- **purpose:** (a) Show the classic signature of overfitting over training time: training error keeps falling while validation error falls, bottoms out and rises; early stopping chooses the validation minimum. (b) Show double descent over model size, so the reader does not confuse the two x-axes: test error rises towards the interpolation threshold and then falls again for larger models.
- **caption:** Figure 2.6: Two views of generalisation. (a) The classic overfitting curve over training time: training error (solid line) keeps falling, while validation error (dashed line) falls, reaches a minimum and rises; early stopping halts training near the minimum. (b) Double descent over model size: test error first follows the classical U-shape, peaks near the size at which the model can just fit every training example exactly (the interpolation threshold), then falls again as the model grows further. Schematic, not to scale; panel (b) after @belkin2019reconciling and @nakkiran2021deep.
- **content:**
  - **Panel (a)** (unchanged from the previous Figure 2.4):
    - x-axis: "Training time (epochs)", no numeric ticks. y-axis: "Error", no numeric ticks. Linear schematic axes.
    - Solid line "Training error": starts high on the left, decreases steeply then more slowly, approaching near zero on the right; monotonically decreasing.
    - Dashed line "Validation error": starts at about the same height, decreases alongside the training line but stays above it, reaches a minimum at roughly 40% of the x-axis, then rises gently to the right.
    - Vertical dotted line at the validation minimum, labelled "early stopping point".
    - Region left of the dotted line lightly shaded and labelled "underfitting → good fit"; region right labelled "overfitting".
    - Bracket between the two lines at the right end labelled "generalisation gap".
  - **Panel (b)** (new):
    - x-axis: "Model size (parameters)", no numeric ticks. y-axis: "Error", no numeric ticks.
    - Solid line "Training error": falls with model size and reaches zero at a vertical dotted line labelled "interpolation threshold", staying at zero to the right.
    - Dashed line "Test error": falls, then rises to a peak at the interpolation threshold (classical U-shape on the left, label "classical regime"), then falls again to the right (label "modern, over-parameterised regime"), ending below the minimum of the left-hand U.
  - No data values in either panel: conceptual illustrations. Panel (a) pattern as described in Goodfellow et al. 2016, sec. 7.8; panel (b) shape as described in Belkin et al. 2019 (Fig. 1) and Nakkiran et al. 2021; no data taken from either.
