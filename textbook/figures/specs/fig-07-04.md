# fig-07-04

- **type:** bar chart
- **purpose:** Show that projected growth in data-centre electricity is concentrated in the United States and China.
- **caption:** Figure 7.4: Data-centre electricity consumption by region, 2024 and 2030 (IEA Base Case). The United States and China account for most of the projected growth. "Rest of world" is the world total minus the three named regions. Source: @iea2026key, Annex A, Table A.4 (data published April 2026).

## content

Grouped vertical bar chart (two bars per region: 2024 solid, 2030 hatched), linear y-axis.

- y-axis: "Electricity consumption (TWh)", linear, 0 to 500, gridlines every 100.
- x-axis: regions in this order.

| Region | 2024 (TWh) | 2030 Base Case (TWh) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | 183 | 426 | iea2026key, Table A.4 (total electricity consumption, Base Case) |
| China | 102 | 277 | iea2026key, Table A.4 |
| Europe | 68 | 114 | iea2026key, Table A.4 |
| Rest of world | 63 | 128 | derived: World (416; 945) minus the three regions above; iea2026key, Table A.4 |

- World totals for reference (show as a note, not as bars): 2024 = 416 TWh; 2030 Base Case = 945 TWh.
- Value labels above each bar.
- Note: "Rest of world" is computed arithmetic from the IEA table (416 − 183 − 102 − 68 = 63; 945 − 426 − 277 − 114 = 128); IEA tables may not sum exactly because of rounding.
