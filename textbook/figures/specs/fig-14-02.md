# fig-14-02

- **type:** diagram (schematic line chart)
- **purpose:** Show that uncertainty about AI widens with time and that published scenarios occupy different regions of a single range of possibilities, rather than competing for one central line.
- **caption:** Figure 14.2: An uncertainty fan for AI capability and impact, 2026–2036 (schematic, not to scale). The fan widens with time. Labelled paths illustrate published views: continued trend extrapolation, a fast acceleration scenario such as AI 2027, a "normal technology" path with slow diffusion, and a plateau if constraints bind. Epoch AI expects power and chip constraints to allow continued scaling to about 2030, and Villalobos and colleagues place the exhaustion of public text between 2026 and 2032, so the plateau path is drawn flattening only after 2030. Diagram by the author, based on [@kokotajlo2025ai], [@narayanan2025ai], [@sevilla2024can] and [@villalobos2024position].
- **content:**
  - Horizontal axis: "Year", marks at 2026, 2028, 2030, 2032, 2034, 2036. No data values.
  - Vertical axis: "AI capability and real-world impact (schematic)", no numbers, no scale.
  - A shaded fan (light grey) starting as a narrow point at 2026 ("today") and widening to the right; darker inner band around the middle.
  - Four labelled paths inside the fan (lines with different dash patterns):
    1. Steepest curve, bending sharply upward from about 2028 and leaving the top of the chart by about 2032 (solid line): label "Fast acceleration (e.g. AI 2027)" placed beside the curve near where it leaves the chart. (A curve that flattens after 2030 would misrepresent the scenario.)
    2. Steady straight-ish rising line through middle: label "Trend continues".
    3. Gentle rise: label "Normal technology: slow diffusion".
    4. Follows (slightly below) the trend line until about 2030, then flattens: label "Plateau after 2030: constraints bind". (Epoch AI's report, sevilla2024can, concludes that scaling to about 2 × 10^29 FLOP is likely feasible through 2030, so the plateau must not start before 2030.) Labels on the right are offset vertically so the three two-line/one-line labels do not touch.
  - Small annotation near the left edge: "Narrow: present is (mostly) known". Near right edge: "Wide: many futures remain possible".
  - Layout: left to right, landscape.
