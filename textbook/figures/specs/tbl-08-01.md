# tbl-08-01

- **type:** table-figure
- **purpose:** Show that reported AI "adoption" varies widely because surveys ask different populations different questions, and that firm-level adoption is lower and shallower than individual use.
- **caption:** Table 8.2: Selected measures of AI adoption and use, 2023–2026, mainly from the United States and Denmark. Differences in wording and population explain much of the variation between sources. The BTOS question changed in November 2025, so rows 3 and 4 are not directly comparable. The May 2026 figure reflects the authors' 2025 reweighting of all survey rounds, which raised the August 2024 estimate from 39.4% to 44.6% (their February 2025 working paper had reported nearly 40% for late 2024); their tracker series is also reweighted. The Danish time saving is from the May 2025 version of the working paper. Anthropic figures are company data and describe conversation patterns, not effects on jobs. Source: bick2026rapid; bick2025state; bick2026what; stlouisfed2026does; humlum2025large; garcialuna2026ai; census2026business; bonney2026microstructure; anthropic2026economic.
