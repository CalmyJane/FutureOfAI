# tbl-08-01

- **type:** table-figure
- **purpose:** Show that reported AI "adoption" varies widely because surveys ask different populations different questions, that use touches only a small share of work hours, and that firm-level adoption is lower and shallower than individual use.
- **caption:** Table 8.2: Selected measures of AI adoption and use, 2023–2026, mainly from the United States and Denmark. Differences in wording and population explain much of the variation between sources. The BTOS question changed in November 2025, so the two BTOS rows are not directly comparable. The Bick et al. figures reflect the authors' 2025 reweighting of all survey rounds; their work-hour shares count non-users. Anthropic figures are company data and describe conversation patterns, not effects on jobs. Source: bick2026rapid; bick2025state; bick2026what; stlouisfed2026does; humlum2025still; garcialuna2026ai; census2026business; bonney2026microstructure; anthropic2026economic.

## content

Table with four columns: **Source (data period)** | **Population** | **Measure** | **Reported value**. Optionally a small horizontal bar in the last column proportional to the percentage (0–100% scale) for the rows that are percentages of a population (rows 1–2 and 4–6); the work-hour, time-saving and Anthropic rows have no bar.

| Source (data period) | Population | Measure | Reported value | Key |
