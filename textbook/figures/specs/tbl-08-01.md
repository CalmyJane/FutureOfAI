# tbl-08-01

- **type:** table-figure
- **purpose:** Show that reported AI "adoption" varies widely because surveys ask different populations different questions, and that firm-level adoption is lower and shallower than individual use.
- **caption:** Table 8.1: Selected measures of AI adoption and use, 2023–2026, mainly from the United States and Denmark. Each row gives the population studied, the measure and the reported value. Differences in wording and population explain much of the variation between sources. The BTOS question changed in November 2025, so rows 3 and 4 are not directly comparable. Bick, Blandin and Deming later revised their survey weighting, raising their August 2024 estimate from 39.4% to 44.6%. Anthropic figures are company data and describe conversation patterns, not effects on jobs. Source: bick2024rapid; bick2025state; humlum2025large; garcialuna2026ai; bonney2026microstructure; anthropic2026economic.
