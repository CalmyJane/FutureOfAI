# fig-01-02

- **type:** bar chart
- **purpose:** Show the sharp drop in ImageNet error when deep learning arrived in 2012, and put the much-quoted "human error" benchmark in context as a single-annotator estimate.
- **caption:** Figure 1.2: Winning top-5 classification error in the ImageNet challenge, 2010–2015 (lower is better). The largest single drop came in 2012, when a deep neural network won for the first time. The dashed line marks one trained human annotator's estimated error of 5.1% on a sample of 1,500 test images; it is a single-person estimate, not a general human benchmark. Source: @russakovsky2015imagenet; @krizhevsky2012imagenet; @he2016deep.
- **content:**
  - x-axis: ILSVRC year (categorical): 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015
  - y-axis: "Top-5 classification error (%)", linear scale, 0 to 30
  - Bars (winning entry, using the provided training data unless noted), label each bar with value and team name:
    - 2010: 28.2 — NEC (source: russakovsky2015imagenet, team tables)
    - 2011: 25.8 — XRCE (source: russakovsky2015imagenet, team tables)
    - 2012: 16.4 — SuperVision / AlexNet (source: russakovsky2015imagenet, team tables). Add a small marker or annotation "15.3 with extra training data" (source: krizhevsky2012imagenet; russakovsky2015imagenet lists 15.32 for SuperVision with outside data)
    - 2013: 11.7 — Clarifai (source: russakovsky2015imagenet, team tables)
    - 2014: 6.7 — GoogLeNet (source: russakovsky2015imagenet, team tables)
    - 2015: 3.57 — ResNet, MSRA (source: he2016deep)
  - Shade bars 2010–2011 lighter ("non-deep methods") and 2012–2015 darker ("deep neural networks"); legend with those two labels.
  - Horizontal dashed line at 5.1 labelled "One annotator, 1,500 images (5.1%)" (source: russakovsky2015imagenet, section 6.4).
  - Optional annotation arrow between 2010 and 2014: "4.2× reduction" (source: russakovsky2015imagenet, section 6.1.1).
