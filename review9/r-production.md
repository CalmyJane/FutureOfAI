### S2. Index builder joins real hyphenated words at line ends, so some pages are left out
- Where: textbook/print/make_index.py:48
- Text now: `body = re.sub(r"(\w)[‐-]\n\s*(\w)", r"\1\2", body)`
- Problem: VERIFIED WRONG (omission). In this PDF, a hyphen added by the line breaker is U+2010 (‐), with 1,802 line ends. A hyphen that belongs to the word is U+002D (-), with 491 line ends. The regex removes both kinds, so "cross-\nentropy" becomes "crossentropy" and no longer matches "cross-entropy". I re-ran the builder's own matching with the two hyphens handled separately. These pages are missing from the printed index:
  - cross-entropy: 99, 120 (printed: 50, 53, 54)
  - self-supervised learning: 99 (printed: 49)
  - chain-of-thought prompting: 105 (printed: 151)

  Words broken across a page turn (for example "Tool‐|former" on pp. 153–154 and "back‐|propagation," on pp. 117–118) are also not matched. None of these changed an entry in this build.
- Evidence: book text from `pdftotext -layout`. For example, p. 99: "…and the loss is the cross-" at a line end. p. 105: "…step-by-step reasoning, chain-of-" at a line end.
- Change to:

  Then rerun make_index.py and build again.
